How to Play GDG Drawing Cam

🎨

1. Choose Google Colors

Pick Blue, Red, Yellow, or Green from the top bar to match the Google Developer Groups logo.

👆

2. Draw on Screen or in the Air

Draw directly with your finger on your phone screen, or point your index finger in the camera to draw in the air!

< >

3. Trace the GDG Logo

Tap the < > icon to toggle the faint logo stencil on/off to help you draw the brackets!

📸

4. Snap & Share to Instagram Story

Tap the shutter button to take the photo with your drawing, then tap Share to Instagram Story and tag @gdg.ensaf!
